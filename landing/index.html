The seven dimensions

The same rubric funders and boards use internally.

Every InstitutionalOS score — whichever lifecycle stage — is built from these seven weighted dimensions, drawn from the InstitutionalOS Health Index Engine spec. Weights below update to match the stage selected above.

75 is the first real permission threshold — not proof of perfection.

InstitutionalOS uses a 25% lift target on every dimension below its ready gate: the next realistic evidence goal, not an all-or-nothing bar. A single unresolved red risk can hold back a "move forward" decision even when the average score looks strong — that check is part of the full guided Review.

What each score means

Every gap sorted, in the same language funders use.

Not Ready

Fix before you apply.
  • No audited or reviewed financials
  • >70% revenue from one source
  • No written bylaws or governance policy
  • No outcome data beyond activity counts

Needs Work

Worth strengthening.
  • Reserves cover 1–3 months
  • Strategic plan exists but is dated
  • Reporting is on time but manual
  • Partnerships exist but aren't formalized

Ready

Looks strong to funders.
  • Audited financials, diversified revenue
  • Active, governed board with written bylaws
  • Outcome tracking tied to mission
  • Named funder relationships and MOUs
