90-Day Improvement Plan

The second half of the loop: execute, review, standardize. One target for the binding constraint, a short list of actions with owners and the evidence that shows each is done, a 15-minute weekly check-in, a monthly check, and a 90-day review. The full evidence-based reassessment comes at six months. The client sees all of it in their portal, can mark actions and log the weekly check-in there, and FIOS shows progress on the deal.
